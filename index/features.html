Core features

The whole swap, start to finish

Every feature exists to answer one question: who on campus can teach me this, and what do I offer back?

Profile

Skill Wallet

List what you can teach and what you want to learn, side by side — your profile doubles as your trade offer.

Match

Smart Matchmaking

We surface people whose "want to learn" lines up with your "can teach," and vice versa — mutual fits first.

Verify

Campus Verification

Sign in with your college email. Every profile on the board belongs to a real student at your school.

Request

Swap Requests

Send a request naming exactly what you'll trade — "I teach photography for 3 sessions of your calculus help."

Talk

Built-in Chat

Once a swap is accepted, chat and schedule sessions without leaving the app or handing out your number.

Plan

Availability Calendar

Mark the hours you're free to teach or learn so matches only land on times that actually work.

Browse

Skill Categories

From coding and languages to music, sport, and design — browse by category or search for exactly what you need.

Trust

Ratings & Badges

After each swap, rate your partner. Reliable teachers earn badges that show up right on their profile.

Track

Swap History

See every skill you've taught and learned on campus, kept in one running log you can look back on.
