Step by step

What Happens At Each Stage

01

Create your profile

Sign up with your campus email, add a photo, and tell the community your year, department, and what you're here for.

~1 day 86% finish this step
02

Add your skills

List what you can teach and what you want to learn, with your level and preferred format — in person, online, or either.

~2 days 83% finish this step
03

Find your match

Smart Matching surfaces students whose skills complement yours. Browse profiles, check ratings, send a request.

~4 days 82% get matched
04

Start learning

Chat to schedule your first session, meet on campus or online, and begin trading knowledge at your own pace.

~9 days 87% begin a session
05

Earn your badge

Wrap up the swap, rate your partner, and collect your badge plus a shareable certificate for your resume.

~21 days 73% finish with a badge
Questions

Process FAQ

What if I don't get matched right away?
Smart Matching scans new profiles daily, so widening your format or skill-level filters usually surfaces a match within a few days.
Why does the funnel narrow so much by the end?
Some students pause mid-swap for exams or breaks — most come back and finish later, which is why the number climbs again term over term.
Can I run more than one swap at once?
Yes — most students run two or three in parallel once they've settled into a rhythm.
