The process

From sign-up to your first swap session.

Five steps, no fees at any point. Here's exactly what happens when you join the board.

Sign up with your college email

We verify you're an actual student at your campus before your profile goes live — no strangers off the internet on the board.

Build your Skill Wallet

List the skills you can teach and the ones you want to learn. Be specific: "conversational Spanish," not just "Spanish."

Browse matches or get matched

Search the board yourself, or let matchmaking surface students whose wants and offers line up with yours.

Send a swap request

Propose the trade in plain terms — what you'll teach, what you want in return, and how many sessions feels fair.

Chat, schedule, and swap

Once accepted, use the built-in chat to pick times on your shared availability, meet up, and start trading skills.
